Nyurix Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 July 2026

Nyurix ("we", "us") is a nutrition-tracking app. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices. Questions or data requests: support@nyurix.app.

Information We Collect

Account information you provide via Google Sign-In (name, email). Health and wellness data you enter: height, weight, age, biological sex, dietary preferences, nutrition goals, and any self-reported medical conditions. Activity data: daily steps (read from Android Health Connect only if you grant permission), sleep, and hydration. Your food logs and the photos you take when scanning meals. We also collect anonymous usage analytics and crash diagnostics to improve reliability.

How We Use Your Data

To provide personalized nutrition tracking, analyze food photos, calculate calorie and macro targets, and show your progress. If you use the optional Friends feature, other users you connect with can see only your streak and consistency score — never your meals, calories, weight, photos, or any other data. We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

Health & Activity Data

If you connect Android Health Connect, we read your daily step count only — no other health records. You can revoke this at any time in Health Connect settings. Self-reported medical conditions are used solely to tailor guidance (for example, avoiding aggressive calorie deficits) and are never used diagnostically or shared. Nyurix is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Supabase (PostgreSQL) protected by row-level security, so each account can access only its own data. Meal and profile photos are stored on Cloudinary. All transmission uses HTTPS. AI credentials are held server-side and never shipped in the app.

Third-Party Services

We use: Google Sign-In (authentication); Groq (AI food-photo analysis using a Meta Llama vision model — your scan photos are processed to estimate nutrition); Supabase (database, authentication, backend functions); Cloudinary (image hosting); Open Food Facts (a public food database queried by the name you type for manual entry); Sentry (crash and error diagnostics); and Expo (app framework and notifications). Each processes data only as needed to provide its function.

Data Retention

You can export all of your data at any time from the Profile screen. We keep account, nutrition, activity, and support data only as long as needed to provide Nyurix, comply with law, resolve disputes, and improve reliability.

Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your account from within the app by going to Profile → Delete Account. This irreversibly removes your login, profile, meals, hydration and weight history, activity data, and stored food photos. To request account deletion outside the app, email support@nyurix.app with the subject "Nyurix Account Deletion Request" and we will process it within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

Nyurix is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected here and, where appropriate, in the app.
